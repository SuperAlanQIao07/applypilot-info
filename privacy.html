ApplyPilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. Operator and scope

This policy describes the personal-use ApplyPilot installation operated by Yinqi Qiao. It covers this installation's use of Google account data and related local application records. It does not describe a public hosted service or the separate services of the upstream ApplyPilot maintainers, employers, or recruiting platforms.

Contact: alan.qiao07@gmail.com.

2. Google data accessed

After explicit OAuth authorization, ApplyPilot requests the Gmail read-only scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly.

It can access the authorized account's email address and Gmail message information, including message and thread identifiers, sender and recipient details, subject lines, timestamps, snippets, and relevant message bodies. The OAuth permission is mailbox-wide read access; the application's search and matching rules narrow its intended use to job applications and recruiting correspondence. It does not download the entire mailbox as a routine operation.

ApplyPilot also receives OAuth access and refresh tokens to access Gmail and renew authorization when permitted. It does not request or store the Gmail password.

3. Purposes of use

Google data is used to match recruiting emails to recorded applications, record application confirmations and outcomes, identify interview or follow-up requests, and display relevant information in private local reports.

For a current authorized application, the application may read a recent, matching verification email and enter its code into that employer's application form. Matching checks include employer/application identity and timing. Ambiguous messages or codes are left for review. This feature does not bypass CAPTCHA, multi-factor authentication, or other access controls.

4. Storage, security, and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in private project-local files with owner-only filesystem permissions. They are not application-level encrypted files. Relevant email identifiers, metadata, snippets, message text, classifications, and application associations may be retained in local SQLite records and private reports or diagnostic evidence. Local backups or exports may contain copies of those records.

Routine verification logs are designed to exclude plaintext verification codes, passwords, and OAuth tokens. Non-secret matching or duplicate-prevention metadata may be retained. API communication uses HTTPS. Access to local records depends on the security of the operator's device and any backups or exports.

There is no automatic deletion period for retained recruiting records. They remain until the operator removes them. Revoking Google access stops future authorized access but does not delete already stored local records. The operator can remove the local OAuth cache and delete retained tracking records, reports, diagnostic evidence, and backups as appropriate. Deletion requests can be sent to the contact address above; deleting local records does not delete employer records or messages in Gmail.

5. Sharing and AI processing

This personal installation does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or marketing, or publish it in a public dashboard. Email data is not shared with upstream project maintainers merely because their code is used.

A verification code is transmitted only to the matching recruiting service to complete the current authorized application verification. Candidate details and application documents separately authorized for submission are sent to the relevant employer or its recruiting service, whose own privacy policy applies.

The configured routine Gmail tracking and code-verification workflows use deterministic rules and do not send email content to AI providers or use it to train AI models. The codebase contains optional AI-assisted email classification; that optional feature is outside this deployment's configured routine workflow. Before enabling it, this policy and the in-product disclosure must identify the provider and data sent, and the account holder must explicitly consent to that additional processing.

Data may be disclosed if legally required. An authorized support or security investigation may involve reviewing relevant local records with the account holder's consent. These exceptions do not permit unrelated use of mailbox data.

6. Control and revocation

The account holder may stop the application or revoke its Google access at any time through Google Account third-party connections. Reauthorization may be necessary if access is later needed again. The read-only Gmail integration cannot send, delete, modify, or label messages.

7. Google API Limited Use

ApplyPilot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes

This policy will be updated if the installation's data practices change. Additional consent will be requested before introducing a new use of Google account data that was not previously disclosed and authorized.
